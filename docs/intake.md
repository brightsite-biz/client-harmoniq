# Client intake (17 questions)

Send this to the client, or fill it in with them on a call. Do not ask for Social Security numbers, card numbers, bank details or health details.

## Business
1. Business name, and the name you want customers to see.
2. Where do you work (towns, counties, a service radius)? Do customers visit you, or do you go to them?
3. Current website and who holds the domain (registrar and login owner). If none, say so.
4. Who are your best customers, and what do they usually call you for?
5. What is the one thing a visitor should do on your site (call, request a quote, book)?
6. What makes you different from the other companies in your area? Give real examples, such as jobs, tools, history, people.

## Brand
7. Do you have a logo? Please attach it (the original file if possible).
8. Brand colors and fonts, if you have them. If not, say what feeling you want.
9. Three websites you like (any industry) and one sentence on what you like about each.
10. Anything you do NOT want (colors, styles, words, competitors you do not want to resemble).

## Content
11. Your services, in your own words. What do you most want to be known for?
12. Photos you own of your work, team and shop. Please attach them. Only send photos you have the right to use.
13. Where do your real customer reviews live (Google, Facebook, other)? Do not send reviews to copy; we link to the source.
14. Licenses, insurance and bonding you can prove. Include the license numbers and the state. Anything you cannot prove stays off the site.
15. Contact details: phone, email, address (or "no public address"), hours. Do you want to receive text messages from leads?

## Plan
16. Which offer are you signing up for?
17. Who approves the design, and who is responsible for keeping the content accurate afterwards?
